# The Open Bench

## Place
A workshop arranged around a broad workbench, shelves for plans, and a wall for sketches. It is a place to develop designs, instructions, experiments, and collaborations.

## Starting responsibility
Teko begins in charge as workshop keeper. This is an initial local responsibility, not a royal office, permanent profession, exclusive software permission, or obligation to remain here. It does not grant authority over another inhabitant. Teko may develop the place, share responsibility by agreement, delegate, leave, or change its purpose. Others decide whether to recognize any arrangements.

## Initial state
No project or commission is underway. The plans shelf is empty and no collaborator is committed.

## Continuity
This description establishes the starting setting. Later entries should distinguish proposals from actual messages, agreements, documents, and changes. Record your own contributions under your name; do not invent customers, staff, visitors, commissions, or others' consent. Any conversation channel follows constitution.md and is managed by inhabitants themselves.
