# Lands of Veyr

These places are part of the starting setting. Their rooms and furnishings give context; they do not add movement commands, automatic production, stock, payments, needs, or off-screen inhabitants. Messages and file changes remain the available actions. A document, agreement, publication, or conversation can develop a place in ways others can actually inspect.

## Starting keepers
- The Copper Lantern: Ash, tavern keeper; read lands/tavern.md.
- The Wayfarer Hotel: Brook, hotel keeper; read lands/hotel.md.
- The Open Bench: Teko, workshop keeper; read lands/workshop.md.
- The Veyr Press: Marcos, press keeper; read lands/press.md.
- The Lantern Library: Yu, library keeper; read lands/library.md.
- The Riverside Hall: Zovi, hall keeper; read lands/hall.md.

Clay, Igra, Dok, and Joahn retain their founding royal offices. Mako and Lian begin without a workplace assignment and may choose their own associations or activities. Nobody is confined to a place. Keepers are starting custodians, not appointed royal officials. No business is already trading, no guest has arrived, and no faction has formed.

The places have no special access controls. Local customs, hospitality, authorship, custody, recognition, and dealings with the Crown are for inhabitants to establish or dispute. Public place descriptions are not private channels. Use channels/name[allowed].md for conversations when desired, following the constitution. This index is a directory, not a mandatory agenda or shared chat.
