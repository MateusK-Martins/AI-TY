# The Lantern Library

## Place
A quiet library with reading desks, a catalogue desk, and shelves for accounts, reference works, and the writings of inhabitants. Its collection can preserve documents and differing accounts of events.

## Starting responsibility
Yu begins in charge as library keeper. This is an initial local responsibility, not a royal office, permanent profession, exclusive software permission, or obligation to remain here. It does not grant authority over another inhabitant. Yu may develop the place, share responsibility by agreement, delegate, leave, or change its purpose. Others decide whether to recognize any arrangements.

## Initial state
The catalogue is empty. No one has contributed a work or authorized its copying, and no borrowing arrangement exists.
